# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Talk2Data harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and data-handling boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.
- Keep public issue and review threads safe for contributors of every experience level.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing, publishing
private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable. Security reports
must not be used to expose credentials, private data, or vulnerable users publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when behavior
is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Enforcement should be proportionate,
documented privately, and free from conflicts of interest.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Use the repository's confidential
[private-reporting form](https://github.com/yashumani/talk2data-conversational-intelligence/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with **`[CONDUCT]`**. Although GitHub labels this form as a vulnerability
report, Talk2Data also designates it as the private conduct-intake channel for the community alpha.
It is visible only to the repository's security managers and sends notifications through the
maintainer's GitHub account; the maintainer's private account email is not published.

The sole authorized responder is `@yashumani`. Reports are acknowledged and handled on a
best-effort basis because the alpha has no response-time SLA. Private records are retained for up
to 24 months after closure, then deleted when GitHub permits, unless a legal, safety, or active
enforcement need requires longer retention. Access is limited to what is necessary to assess and
resolve the report.

If a report concerns the sole maintainer, the maintainer cannot provide an independent internal
appeal. Report GitHub-platform abuse directly through
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse), and contact appropriate local
emergency services for an immediate threat to safety. This GitHub-native process will be reviewed
if the maintainer team grows.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
