# Support

Talk2Data is an alpha project maintained on a best-effort basis.

- Use the support-question issue form for installation, synthetic demo, documentation, or
  contributor workflow questions.
- Use the bug form for reproducible defects and the feature form for bounded proposals.
- Never put credentials, private data, employer information, production logs, or vulnerability
  details in a public issue.
- Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities and
  [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for conduct incidents.

Maintainers do not provide an SLA, production hosting, data recovery, legal advice, or enterprise
support through the community repository. A public Pages gallery demonstrates visualizations only;
it is not a support or data-processing endpoint.
